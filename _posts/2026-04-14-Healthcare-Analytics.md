### Project Overview
End-to-end healthcare analytics project analyzing hospital performance, patient outcomes, and operational KPIs across 15 Saudi Arabian hospitals. Built with **Python**, **Power BI**, and **SQL**, aligned with **Saudi Vision 2030** healthcare transformation goals.

### Key Findings
* **Analyzed 3,000+ admissions** and 5,000+ treatments across 15 hospitals.
* **Calculated 12 industry-standard** healthcare KPIs.
* **Identified Hajj season impact** on hospital capacity (7.4% increase).
* **Compared Government vs Private** hospital performance.
* **Built interactive 4-page Power BI dashboard**.

### Tools & Technologies
* **Python:** `pandas`, `matplotlib`, `seaborn`, `numpy`
* **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard with DAX measures
* **SQL:** Complex queries with JOINs, CTEs, Window Functions
* **Excel:** Multi-sheet report with pivot tables

### Healthcare KPIs Tracked

| KPI | Value | Benchmark | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Average Length of Stay** | 7.97 days | 4-5 days | ⚠️ High |
| **Bed Occupancy Rate** | 78.7% | 80-85% | ⬇️ Low |
| **30-Day Readmission Rate** | 14.9% | <10% | ⚠️ High |
| **Patient Satisfaction** | 3.86/5 | >4.0 | 👍 Good |

### Dataset Description
Detailed breakdown of the multi-dimensional dataset used for this analysis:
* **15 Hospitals:** A diverse mix of facilities across various regions in Saudi Arabia.
* **500 Patients:** Detailed demographics including age, gender, and nationality.
* **3,000 Admission Records:** Complete tracking from check-in to discharge.
* **5,000 Treatment Records:** Documented medical procedures and clinical interventions.
* **4,000 Lab Results:** Integrated laboratory data for comprehensive clinical insights.
* **2,000 Satisfaction Surveys:** Patient-reported experience measures (PREMs).
